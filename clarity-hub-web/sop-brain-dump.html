The Process Brain Dump

Turn a process in your head into a clean, structured outline — in under 10 minutes.

Use these 7 prompts as a guide Hide
  1. What is this process called? The name you’d use when telling someone “I’ll handle the…”
  2. What triggers it? Booking, email, recurring Monday — what kicks it off
  3. How often do you do it? Daily, weekly, once per client, once a quarter
  4. Who’s involved? You, clients, team, tools. If it’s just you, say so
  5. What does “done” look like? The deliverable, signal, or outcome
  6. Walk me through what you actually do. Step by step. Include the bits you’d normally skip
  7. Where does it usually go wrong? Failure points. What you keep forgetting
