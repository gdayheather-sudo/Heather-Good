Privacy Policy

How we look after your information.

The Clarity Hub (“we”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through clarityhub.com.au, how we use it, and the choices you have. We operate in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

We only collect information you give us directly through one of our forms:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business name (optional)
  • Any message or notes you choose to share

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or cross‑site tracking. For aggregate page‑view statistics we use Umami, a privacy‑focused analytics service that doesn’t set cookies, doesn’t track visitors across other sites, and doesn’t store personally identifying information.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry
  • To contact you about a waiting list you’ve joined
  • To tell you when a programme, tool, or resource you’ve expressed interest in becomes available

We will not sell your information, share it with unrelated third parties, or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it’s stored

Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspree and reach us by email via Microsoft 365. Both providers process data in line with their own privacy policies and applicable international data‑protection laws.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as needed to respond to you or keep you on a waiting list. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Under Australian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that’s wrong
  • Ask us to delete it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (e.g. unsubscribe from a waiting list)

Email hello@clarityhub.com.au for any of these. We’ll respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies & analytics

This site doesn’t set cookies. Our analytics provider (Umami) and our hosting provider (Vercel) may log basic request information — such as page URL, referrer, browser type, and anonymised country — for security, performance, and aggregate analytics. None of this data is used to identify individual visitors.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the “Last updated” date below. Material changes that affect how we use your information will be communicated to anyone currently on a waiting list.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or complaints about how we handle your information:
hello@clarityhub.com.au

If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
