About

Heather Good

Founder, The Clarity Hub.

The Clarity Hub started with a simple observation: most of the founders Heather was working with were capable, driven, and delivering real results — but they were doing it from inside their head. Everything tracked in memory. Decisions made on instinct. Visibility near zero.

She built The Clarity Hub to change that. Not with more hustle or heavier tools, but with practical systems, AI‑supported structure, and thoughtful organisational support that makes growth feel less reactive.

Heather works with founders, operators, and small‑team service businesses who know their business can be calmer — and want the structure to prove it.
