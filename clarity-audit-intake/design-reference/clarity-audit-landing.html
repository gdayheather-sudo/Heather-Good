Questions

Things people ask.

Why "founding rate"?
I'm testing the audit format with the first five founders. You get the same depth of work — at a lower price — in exchange for honest feedback that helps me refine it. After the first five, the price moves to $497.
Do I need to know anything about AI before the call?
Not at all. Some of the best audits I do are with founders who've barely touched it. We meet you where you are — the goal is to identify where AI actually helps you, not to add complexity for the sake of it.
What if my "systems" are just chaos?
That's exactly the right starting point. The audit is designed for founders who don't have systems yet — or whose systems have grown organically and need a fresh look.
How is this different from a business coach?
A business coach helps you think. I help you build. The audit is operationally focused: tools, workflows, AI integration, what to automate, what to kill. Less mindset, more architecture.
Can I get a refund if it's not useful?
Yes. If you don't walk away from the call with at least three concrete actions that will save you time or money — I'll refund the call in full. No drama.
When are the calls available?
Weeknights from 6:30pm and Saturday mornings (AEST). Once you apply, I'll send you a booking link with available windows.
